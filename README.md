# 🍽️ Gursha

### Ethiopia's AI-Powered Nutrition & Wellness Intelligence Platform

**Transforming Ethiopian meals into personalized wellness intelligence.**

---

## 📖 About Gursha

Gursha is an AI-powered nutrition and wellness platform built specifically for Ethiopian foods, lifestyles, and health challenges.

Unlike traditional calorie-counting applications, Gursha helps users understand how their daily meals affect their overall health, energy levels, fitness performance, mental focus, and long-term wellness.

Using Computer Vision, Nutrition Intelligence, Predictive Analytics, and Personalized AI Recommendations, Gursha empowers Ethiopians to make healthier decisions every day.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Millions of Ethiopians consume traditional meals daily but lack access to tools that explain:

- Nutritional composition of meals
- Protein intake
- Vitamin deficiencies
- Iron deficiencies
- Hydration levels
- Long-term nutrition risks

Many global nutrition applications are designed for Western foods and do not accurately recognize Ethiopian meals.

### Key Challenges

- Over 60,000 deaths annually in Ethiopia are associated with dietary risk factors.
- Nutrition-related diseases continue to affect millions across Africa.
- Students struggle with focus, energy, and healthy eating habits.
- Gym users often consume supplements without understanding overall nutritional needs.
- Many people know food affects health but do not understand nutrient deficiencies developing over time.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Gursha converts Ethiopian meals into actionable wellness intelligence.

Users can:

1. Upload a meal photo.
2. Let AI recognize the food.
3. Confirm meal portions.
4. Receive nutrition analysis.
5. Get personalized wellness recommendations.
6. Track long-term health trends.
7. Improve nutrition through AI guidance.

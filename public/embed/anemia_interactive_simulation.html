What is anemia?

Anemia is a condition where your blood doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells (RBCs) or hemoglobin to carry adequate oxygen to body tissues. Think of it as a delivery failure — your cells are starved of the oxygen they need to function.

The role of hemoglobin

Hemoglobin is the protein inside red blood cells that binds oxygen in the lungs and releases it to tissues. When hemoglobin levels fall — for any of the three reasons above — every organ in your body receives less oxygen, triggering the cascade of anemia symptoms.

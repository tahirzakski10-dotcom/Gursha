📈 Blood Glucose Graph

How it works: After a meal, carbohydrates break down into glucose in the small intestine. As blood glucose rises, pancreatic beta cells detect this and release insulin. Insulin acts like a "key" unlocking cell receptors, allowing glucose to enter muscle, liver, and fat cells. Blood sugar returns to the normal range of 70–100 mg/dL.
